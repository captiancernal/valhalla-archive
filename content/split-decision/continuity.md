## Rules learned from the pilot
- The tracker has no per-game player stats. Columns talk about results, margins, opponents, records and season-level facts only.
- Season stat totals (yards, awards, All-Americans) only appear in a season's **final** column. In-season columns may name players with their position, class and preseason rating from the depth chart.
- Every "N-N" in a column must be a real score or a record at that point in the season (`py -P scripts/columns.py check <season>` enforces this). Write vote counts and hypotheticals in words ("six to one"). For other real pairs (e.g. a conference record), add `allowed_pairs: ["5-4"]` to the front matter after checking the data.
- Ken's "Lil" explanation changes every time and never reveals the anagram.
- Weave one or two world details into each take; rotate places and people; don't repeat the same pairing two weeks running.
