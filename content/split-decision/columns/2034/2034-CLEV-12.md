## Mickey

VIKINGS 70, IOWA STATE 27. TWELVE AND OH.

Forty-three points of daylight. Our fifth win over the Cyclones in five tries. And our third perfect regular season in five years: 2030, 2031, and now 2034. Folks, I've been doing this since 1994. I didn't know a team could do this three times. I'm told it's allowed.

Nine and oh in the Big 12. And the RedHawks are nine and oh too. Undefeated. Scoring more points than any team I've ever seen. We play them in the Big 12 championship game. We haven't played them since they beat us in the playoff quarterfinal two years ago.

Thanksgiving was at our house. It was the first Thanksgiving without Viking in fifteen years. Rose, three and a half, walked into the house and asked, "Where's Viking?" Bridget knelt down and explained. Rose thought about it for a long time. Then she went to the backyard, by herself, in her coat, and stood by the little walnut marker under the maple, and waved the Vikings flag at it. Just once. Then she came inside and asked for pie.

Folks, I don't have anything else to say about that. I'm just going to let it sit there.

Five pierogi. One on the plate. One for Rose, who's allowed.

## Ken
