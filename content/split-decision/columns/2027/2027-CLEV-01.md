## Mickey

Year four, friends. The board at Big Stan's has a third panel now, and the newest name painted on it is **The Hocker**: brisket, a fried egg, hot peppers and pierogi, all on a Kaiser roll "with a cannon arm of mustard." Stan hung it the week before the opener and said a short prayer over it. Chester bowed his head. I'm not sure Chester knew what we were praying for, but he's a team player.

Then our guys opened at home and beat Toledo 45-24. Twenty-one points of daylight, and Amar'e Hocker, a junior now and rated 86, looked like the best quarterback I've covered in thirty-four years. That's jersey thirty-four in the closet, by the way. Donna has stopped commenting on the closet. That's how I know she's worried.

And now I need to tell you about our freshman receiver, the number twenty-three recruit in the country, out of Minnesota. His name is Kenny. Kenny Tongues. A Kenny, folks, who *catches things*. A Kenny who *believes*. I've already started calling him "the good Kenny."

Four pierogi. Put it in the time capsule.

## Ken

I'm aware of the freshman. I'm aware of what Mickey's going to do with the freshman. I'd like the record to show that I've never dropped a pass in my life, mostly because I've never been thrown one.

A twenty-one-point win over Toledo to open. Fine. Efficient. The Vikings lost Jayden Bellamy to the draft, a second-round pick, plus a big chunk of last year's defense, and the offense is now built around a junior quarterback and a freshman receiver who, for the moment, shares my name and none of my anxieties.

At the Lantern Room, Nadia had redecorated. The RedHawks bobblehead now has a companion: a small gold trophy she bought online, labeled "2027 — WE'LL SEE." She says Miami (OH) is "built different this year." I believe her, which is the problem.

The Doom Model returned from the offseason in a better mood. I'm not sure I trust a spreadsheet that took a vacation.

"Lil": a ventriloquist's dummy at my sixth birthday party. Its name was Ken. I was the smaller one.

## Back and forth

## Final Verdict

The Hocker on the board, a junior quarterback in command, and a freshman Kenny who, for now, has all the belief in the family.
