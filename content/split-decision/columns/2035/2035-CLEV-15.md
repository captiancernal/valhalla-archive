## Mickey

VIKINGS 48, OHIO 40. INTO THE NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP GAME.

Eight points. Our second straight semifinal win over Ohio. Our third win over them in twelve months. And our sixth trip to the national championship game, folks: 2028, 2029, 2031, 2034 and now 2035. That's five. Kenny's correcting me. Five. Five trips. I'm emotional. I miscounted.

Rose waved the sad flag again. Bridget sent a picture: Rose asleep on Eli's shoulder, holding the Ohio flag this time, because Ohio was sad. Bridget wrote, "she waved it for us, dad. she's proud of you anyway. so am i. go win it." Then she wrote, "for somebody from ohio."

And now it's the RedHawks. The defending champions. Undefeated. The team that beat us twice last year, including the title game, and beat us at home three weeks ago by twenty-eight. Nadia's team.

Donna is going to the game. She's sitting with Nadia. She's bringing the cape. She said, "Michael, it's a neutral site. The cape works on neutral sites." I said, "Donna, the cape lost to the RedHawks in last year's title game." She said, "The cape has never been to a game like this one." I don't know what she means. I believe her anyway.

Five pierogi.

## Ken

An eight-point semifinal win over Ohio. Fourteen and one. Fifth trip to the national championship game in eight years.

Since 2024, I've been keeping a list I didn't tell Mickey about. It's titled "Things That Don't Happen." The first entry was "The Vikings beat Ohio in Cleveland." It happened in 2030.
