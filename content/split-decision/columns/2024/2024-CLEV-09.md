## Mickey

I'm not going to sugarcoat it, and you know how much I love sugarcoating.

Ohio 49, Vikings 24, in our own building. Tim Slocombe's Bobcats came up the road and played like a team that had been waiting all year to ruin my Saturday, and friends, they ruined it thoroughly and with excellent fundamentals.

We're 5-4. And here's where I'll be the believer you pay me to be: this was the first time the Dolan Vikings and the Slocombe Bobcats met in this new chapter, and every great rivalry starts with somebody getting embarrassed. Somebody has to be the "before" picture. This year, it was us. That just means the "after" picture is going to be *glorious*.

The second seat went to a twelve-year-old named Marcus whose dad works nights. He wore a cardboard helmet and cheered every first down like it was a touchdown, even at the end. That kid is a Viking. I want him on the coaching staff.

Zero pierogi. I gave Marcus my last one.

## Ken

There are losses, and there are lessons, and then there's whatever that was.

A twenty-five-point loss at home to Ohio. The Bobcats are now living rent-free in my spreadsheet, and I'm not even mad, which is the scary part. They're good. They're organized. They looked like a program that knows exactly who it is, and on Saturday the Vikings looked like a program that's still introducing itself.

Here's the scar tissue talking: this is how it starts. You start a season hot, you win a few close ones, people buy jerseys, and then a disciplined rival walks in and reminds everyone of the order of things. I've seen this movie. I know the ending. The ending is a lot of people saying "next year."

The Doom Model, for the record, called this one by a lot. I'm not celebrating. I'm just updating the spreadsheet with the solemnity it deserves.
