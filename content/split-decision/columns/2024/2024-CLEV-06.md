## Mickey

THAT'S A VIKING, FOLKS.

One week after Lubbock, our guys went down to Dallas, walked into SMU's house and came out with a 28-22 win. Road win. Bounce-back win. The kind of win that tells you who a team really is, and friends, this team is *ours*.

We're 4-2. You know what I loved? The response. Last week Kenny was ready to sell the furniture. This week the Vikings went on the road, against a good team, and won a six-point game. Six points! That's a touchdown and a missed extra point. That's character with a little margin for error.

The second seat went to a retired shop teacher named Walt who brought a thermos of coffee so strong it should be regulated. He said, "Teams are like engines. You find out what they are when they're cold." This engine turned over, friends.

Four pierogi, and I'm going to frame the box score next to my 1990 diploma.

## Ken

Fine. Good. Wow.

That's the column. Marlene says I need two hundred words, so I'll elaborate on "wow."

A road win at SMU after a road loss in Lubbock is genuinely encouraging, and I hate typing "genuinely encouraging" because it feels like signing something. The defense held a decent team to twenty-two, the second straight week under twenty-five. That's a trend. I don't like trends I can't argue with.

My concern, because I always have one, is the schedule. The Vikings have now seen what a Big 12 road game feels like twice, and they're one and one. Colorado is next, on the road again, at altitude. Thin air, thick crowds.

The Doom Model has quietly lowered its loss projection for the season. I didn't approve that. It made the change on its own. Like a teenager.
