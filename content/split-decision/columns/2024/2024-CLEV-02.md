## Mickey

Okay. Okay, okay, okay.

Kansas State came into our house and left with a 42-31 win, and I'm not going to pretend that didn't sting like a paper cut on a Friday. We're 1-1. But listen to me, friends, because this is important: we scored thirty-one points against a Big 12 power in the second game of a brand-new staff. Thirty-one! Last decade I'd have thrown a parade for thirty-one.

My second seat went to a mail carrier named Dwight who's delivered to the same block for twenty-two years. He told me the secret to his job is "you keep showing up." Dwight gets it. You lose one, you put the boots back on.

Harold Fannin Jr. is a 95-overall tight end, and I'd hand him the keys to my car, my house and Donna's lasagna recipe, which I am not allowed to share. Zero pierogi this week. A moment of silence, then lunch.

## Ken

There it is.

Forty-two points allowed at home to Kansas State. I'm not going to say "I told you so," because I write for a family newspaper and Marlene has asked me to "grow." I'll just note that last week's victory parade was routed past a pothole.

Here's the actual problem, and it isn't scoring; Mickey is right that the offense showed up. It's that a defense which gives up forty-two at home in September tends to give up forty-two on the road in November, and the road is a meaner place.

I'm not panicking. Panic is for people who expected something. I expected this. That's my whole system.

A reader asked about "Lil" again. It was a cousin, also Ken, who was taller. Family thing. Please stop writing in.

## Back and forth

**Mickey:** Thirty-one points against K-State!

**Ken:** And forty-two the other way. I'm a numbers guy; I read both columns.

## Final Verdict

The offense is real, the defense is a rumor, and Ken slept like a baby, which is to say he woke up every two hours to check the Doom Model.
