## Mickey

LIBERTY BOWL CHAMPIONS. Say it slow, folks. *Liberty. Bowl. Champions.*

Vikings 31, Army 21, and the first season of the Josh Dolan era ends at 9-4 with a trophy on the bus. Nine wins! A bowl win in year one! I've been covering this program since before some of these players' parents could drive, and I've never had a December like this one.

And the guys, my people, the guys. Connor Bazelak threw for 3,962 yards this season. Terion Stewart ran for 1,098. And Harold Fannin Jr., our 95-overall tight end, caught for 1,119 yards, won the Mackey Award as the nation's best tight end, *and* made All-American. A Mackey winner in a Vikings uniform. I need to sit down. I am sitting down. I need to sit down *more*.

The second seat went to Gloria, the crossing guard from opening night. Full circle, friends. She said, "I told you." She did not tell me. I'll allow it.

Five pierogi. Jersey thirty-one gets a gold star.

## Ken

Respectfully, wow.

Nine wins. A bowl trophy. A Mackey Award. A first-team All-American. I came into this season with a spreadsheet, a fake plant and a firm commitment to disappointment, and I'm leaving with a ten-point bowl win over Army and a strange warm feeling I've been told is "satisfaction."

Let me give the honest summary, because I owe it. The Vikings finished 5-4 in the Big 12, fifth in the conference, unranked in the final poll. There are real things to fix, starting with the night Ohio came to town. But a first-year staff that goes 9-4, wins three one-score road games, beats Miami (OH) in a shootout and wins its bowl has built something.

I'm not saying I believe. I'm saying I've opened the door a crack. The Doom Model has been put on administrative leave. Pops would have said "never trust a lead." Pops never saw a Mackey winner in green.
