## Mickey

Friends, I have waited thirty years to write this sentence: the Josh Dolan era is undefeated.

Down in New Orleans, on the road, against a Tulane team that wanted no part of a quiet August night, our guys walked out with a 24-21 win. Three points. Do you know what three points is? It's a field goal. It's the width of a hot dog. It's the difference between a long bus ride and a *happy* long bus ride, and folks, that bus was singing.

I gave my spare seat this week to Gloria, a retired crossing guard who has stopped traffic for children for forty years. She'd never seen a college game. She cried at the anthem, and I cried because she cried.

That's jersey number thirty-one in the closet, and Donna has *questions*. Put it in the time capsule. Three pierogi, and a fourth in the freezer for later.

## Ken

Respectfully, no.

We beat Tulane by a field goal. That's not a coronation, that's a coin flip that landed heads. I've reviewed the result, I've reviewed the margin, and I've reviewed Mickey, who is currently wearing a plastic helmet indoors.

The Doom Model had this one as a loss. The Doom Model is a cautious instrument; it doesn't panic over one data point, and neither should you. One game is a sample size of one. One game is a rumor.

What I will grant: the Vikings didn't fold on the road, and a team that closes out a three-point game in August might, theoretically, close out one in November. Might. Ask me in January.

Also, a reader asked why I'm called "Lil." It's from a youth-league coach who couldn't pronounce "Kenneth." It's not interesting. Moving on.
