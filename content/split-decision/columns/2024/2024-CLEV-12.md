## Mickey

I need you to understand that I am typing this with one hand, because the other hand is still holding a towel I waved for four straight quarters.

VIKINGS 46, MIAMI (OH) 45. In Oxford. On the road. Against Joe Snyderwine's RedHawks, who score points the way I eat pierogi: constantly, and with no plan to stop.

One point, friends. ONE. That's the smallest possible amount of winning, and it's the most beautiful amount I've ever seen. We're 8-4. Eight wins in year one of the Dolan era, with a rivalry win to close the regular season. I don't know what else you want from a football team. I don't know what else you want from *life*.

The second seat went to Donna. My wife. She has been to four games in thirty-one years of marriage. She watched this one, stood up for the final play, and said, "Michael, I think I understand now." I wept, folks. Openly. In a parking garage.

Five pierogi. The full Pierogi Index. Put it in the time capsule and bury it under the lake.
