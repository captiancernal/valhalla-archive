    How it works
    • Pick a square, then type a player who fits both its row and its column. A wrong guess still uses one of your twelve.
    • Programs grids put Cleveland State, Ohio and Miami (OH) across the top. Open grids mix any conditions, so they lean on Cleveland State, which has the deepest records.
    • "1 of 14" under a name means fourteen players would have fit that square.
    • Conditions cover positions, national awards, All-American teams, team stat leaders, seasons played, recruit stars, home state, transfers, the draft and peak ratings.

    Every square has at least two possible answers, and every grid can be completed with nine different players. The daily grid is the same for everyone and alternates between Programs and Open grids.
